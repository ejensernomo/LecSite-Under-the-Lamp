About Under The Lamp Coffee

OUR STORY

It Started With A Conversation.

Under the Lamp was born out of a love for those late-night deep conversations, creative breakthroughs, and cozy, impromptu jam sessions. We wanted to capture that exact feeling and bring it into the daytime.

That’s why we designed our shop to feel less like a rigid commercial café and more like an extension of your own backyard — relaxed, familiar, and completely open to everyone.

Every corner of UTL is meant to encourage you to stay a little longer, talk a little deeper, and enjoy the moment you're in.

Drinks Made
For Every Mood.

We believe that a great drink is the ultimate catalyst for connection. That’s why our bar is dedicated to serving unique flavor profiles you won’t find anywhere else.

Whether you're a coffee purist or looking for something sweet, we treat every drink as an opportunity to create something memorable.

Made With Care

From familiar favorites to unique creations, every drink is part of the UTL experience.
